# Control Theory and Quant Finance

Control theory and quantitative finance share a common language: models, predictions, and feedback. This article explores how control frameworks can clarify algorithmic trading and risk systems.

## State estimation and filtering

Kalman filters and state estimators are familiar to control engineers. In quant finance, similar estimators are used to infer latent factors, volatility, and regime changes from noisy market data.

## Feedback and adaptation

Trading systems can be viewed as control loops where position sizes are manipulated to keep risk and returns within target bands. Adaptive rules and portfolio rebalancing mimic closed-loop regulation.

## Optimisation under constraints

Control systems routinely balance objectives with constraints. Quant strategies face the same trade-offs: transaction costs, leverage limits, and liquidity constraints all shape the optimal action.
