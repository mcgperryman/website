# Spectral Estimation Notebook

A notebook-based exploration of spectral estimation techniques for system identification and noise analysis.

## Topics covered

- Fourier spectra of noisy signals
- Windowing and resolution trade-offs
- Peak detection for dominant frequencies
- Practical filtering for measurement noise

## Purpose

This project is designed to be a reproducible reference for anyone building estimation workflows in signal-rich systems such as control loops or trading signals.
